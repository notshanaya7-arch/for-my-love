a letter from Shanaya for Yash

Dear Yash,

I still think it is a little funny that out of all the people in the world, out of all the places we could have met, somehow we found each other in Minecraft.

It started in a world made of blocks and pixels, something that was probably supposed to be just another ordinary day, and somehow that tiny beginning turned into something that means so much more to me. I never expected meeting you would become such an important part of my life.

I never expected a random conversation to turn into late-night talks, inside jokes, little memories, missing each other, caring about each other's days, and eventually this feeling that no matter how far away you are, you still feel incredibly close to me.

There are so many moments when I wish distance wasn't a thing. I wish I could just turn around and find you next to me. I want the ordinary things with you — sitting together, talking about absolutely nothing, laughing at something stupid, going somewhere just because we can, and having those quiet moments where we don't even have to say anything.

Sometimes the distance feels unfair because I don't want a screen between us; I want you here. I want to make real memories with you instead of imagining what those memories will feel like.

But even with all those miles between us, I hope you know how much you mean to me. You have become someone I look for, someone I think about, someone whose messages can completely change my mood, and someone I genuinely cannot imagine my life without anymore.

I love the little things about you, the things you probably don't even realise I notice. I love the way you make me smile, the way you make ordinary conversations feel special, and the way somehow you can make me feel close to you even when you're far away.

And more than anything, I love us. I love that our story didn't begin in some perfect movie moment. It began somewhere completely unexpected, somewhere simple, somewhere we probably never would have imagined would become the beginning of this.

A Minecraft world gave me a person I now care about more than I know how to explain. And maybe that is my favorite part of our story — that something so random became something so real.

So yes, I miss you. A lot. I hate the distance sometimes. I hate having to count the time until we can finally be in the same place. But I would still choose you.

Through the waiting, through the miles, through every moment where I wish you were here, I would still choose you. Because you are worth the distance, worth the waiting, and worth every mile between us.

Until the day I don't have to miss you anymore, until the day I can actually be beside you instead of writing these words from far away, I hope you always remember one thing:

There is a girl named Shanaya who loves you very, very much, who is incredibly grateful that a random Minecraft world somehow led her to you, and who cannot wait to see what the next chapter of our story looks like.

I love you, Yash. More than these words can fit on a page.

Love,
Shanaya ♥
